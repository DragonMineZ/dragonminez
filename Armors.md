# Armors

DMZ adds dozens of character-themed **armor sets** (Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo, Bardock, Trunks, the Androids…). They aren't crafted at a vanilla crafting table — they're assembled at a **Kikono Station**, which needs **Star Energy** to run.

## What you need

| Thing | Role |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Kikono Station** (block) | The armor-crafting machine. Holds up to **2,000 Star Energy**. |
| **Fuel Generator** (block) | Burns fuel to produce **Star Energy** (holds up to 1,000). |
| **Energy Cable** (block) | Pipes energy from the generator to the station. |
| **Kikono materials** | **Kikono Cloth** and **Kikono String** — the crafting materials (made from **Kikono Shards**, mined as **Kikono Ore** on [[Planet Namek|Dimension-Namek]]). |
| **Armor pattern** | The blueprint that decides *which* armor you make (e.g. the Goku pattern → Goku armor). |
| **Template** | A vanilla armor piece of the matching slot (Iron Chestplate / Leggings / Boots) used as the base. |

## Energy — Star Energy

The Station runs on **Star Energy**, which you generate yourself:

1. Place a **Fuel Generator** and put **any furnace fuel** in it (coal, charcoal, wood, blaze rods, lava buckets…). It burns fuel just like a furnace, filling its energy buffer.
2. Connect the generator to the **Kikono Station** with **Energy Cables**.
3. The Station draws the energy it needs from the network to craft.

Each armor piece costs about **1,000 Star Energy** and a short crafting time by default (tunable per recipe).

## Procedure

1. Mine **Kikono Ore** on Namek → **Kikono Shards** → craft **Kikono Cloth** and **Kikono String**.
2. Set up a **Fuel Generator + Energy Cable → Kikono Station** and fuel the generator so Star Energy flows to the Station.
3. Open the **Kikono Station** and load:
   - the **pattern** (blueprint) for the armor you want,
   - the **template** (matching Iron armor piece),
   - the **Kikono materials** (Cloth + String).
4. With enough Star Energy, the Station crafts the piece. Repeat for chestplate, leggings and boots (some sets also include a helmet).

## What armor does — defense & resistance

DMZ armor is real protective gear: each piece provides **defense (protection), toughness and knockback resistance**.

This **stacks on top of your innate Resistance (RES) stat**. Remember RES is your character's built-in defense — it drives your damage mitigation, max Stamina and Poise (see [[Stats & Attributes|Stats-and-Attributes]]). Worn armor adds *extra* defense and toughness on top, so a well-geared fighter takes noticeably less damage and is harder to stagger. See [[Combat|Combat]] for how mitigation and poise work in fights.

> The special **Strongest Armor** and **Invincible Armor** sets are exceptions — they're not crafted, they're **dragon wishes** (see [[Dragons & Wishes|Dragons-and-Wishes]]).
