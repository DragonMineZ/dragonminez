# Hyperbolic Time Chamber

The Room of Spirit and Time — a boundless white void built for one thing: **training**. Dimension id: `dragonminez:time_chamber`.

## Gravity

**×10 by default** — the highest ambient gravity of any DMZ dimension. Everything is heavier here: movement, jumping and flight are all weighed down, which is exactly what makes it a training ground. (Tunable in [[General Server|general-server]] / [[Gravity System|Gravity-System]].)

## Environment

The whole dimension is a flat, endless expanse of **Time Chamber Block** under a blank sky — there are **no biomes, no ores, no natural mobs and no structures**. You bring your own training.

## Purpose — training

The Chamber is the premier place to grind **Training Points** and **form mastery**:

- Its heavy gravity and dedicated training bonuses massively boost gains.
- Combine it with **[[Weights|Weights]]** to push your load ratio into the ideal/heavy zone.

See **[[Training Points & Mastery|Training-Points-and-Mastery]]** for how training multipliers stack.

## Getting there

Enter through the **Time Chamber Portal** found in the Time Chamber structure (at **Kami's Lookout**). See [[Structures|Structures]].
