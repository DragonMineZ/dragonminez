# Planet Namek

The home planet of the Namekians — a green-skied world of Ajissa forests and blue waters. Dimension id: `dragonminez:namek`.

## Gravity

**Normal (×1.0)** by default — same as the Overworld (tunable in [[General Server|general-server]] / [[Gravity System|Gravity-System]]).

## Biomes

- **Ajissa Plains**
- **Sacred Land** (the Grand Elder's region)
- **Namekian Rivers**

## Ores & resources

Namek has the **full ore set** embedded in Namek stone (and deepslate variants): coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond and emerald — so it's a viable place to mine vanilla resources.

Its exclusive resource is **Kikono Ore**, which drops **Kikono Shards** — the base material for **Kikono gear** (Kikono Stick, String, Cloth and the armor-crafting recipes).

There's also a **low chance** of finding **Gete Debris** scattered underground — a rare ore (generated like Ancient Debris, in small veins) tied to the Gete-tech/Metal-Cooler machinery.

## Entities

- **Namek Frog** (and the rare **Ginyu** variant) — passive wildlife.
- **Namekians** — Villager/Trader (general goods) and **Namek Warriors** (village defenders).
- **CC-Namekian** — sells treasure maps to Namek structures (see [[Bestiary-NPCs|Bestiary-NPCs]]).
- **Frieza Soldiers** — hostile invaders (see [[Bestiary-Hostiles|Bestiary-Hostiles]]).
- Plus vanilla water life (fish, squid) in the rivers.

## Structures

- **Frieza's Ship** — in the Ajissa Plains.
- **Grand Elder's House** (Elder Guru) — in the Sacred Land.

## Getting there

Travel by **Space Pod** — see [[Space Pod Destinations|Space-Pod-Destinations]].
