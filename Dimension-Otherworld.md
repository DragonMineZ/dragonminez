# Otherworld

The afterlife. When you die (with the Otherworld system enabled) you don't just respawn — your spirit travels to the Otherworld, marked with a **halo**, until you're revived. Dimension id: `dragonminez:otherworld`.

## Gravity

**Normal (×1.0)** by default (tunable in [[General Server|general-server]] / [[Gravity System|Gravity-System]]).

## Environment

A serene realm of clouds built from **Otherworld Cloud** blocks — you walk among the heavens. It has **no ores, no natural mob spawns and no generated structures**; it's a gameplay space, not a place to gather resources.

## Purpose — death & revival

Otherworld is the backbone of DMZ's death system:

- On death you arrive here as a haloed spirit instead of respawning normally.
- You can be **revived** back to the living world (e.g. via a dragon wish, or admin commands like `/dmzrevive`; the halo is managed with `/dmzhalo`). See [[Commands|Commands]] and [[Dragons & Wishes|Dragons-and-Wishes]].

The whole system can be switched off with the **`otherworldActive`** server toggle — if disabled, the Otherworld doesn't exist and vanilla respawn is used instead (see [[General Server|general-server]]).
