# Sacred Planet

The Sacred World of the Kais — a lush, otherworldly planet home to the Kais. Dimension id: `dragonminez:sacredkaiplanet`.

## Gravity

**Normal (×1.0)** by default (tunable in [[General Server|general-server]] / [[Gravity System|Gravity-System]]).

## Biomes

- **Sacred Plains**
- **Sacred Hills**
- **Sacred Rivers**

## Blocks & resources

The Sacred Planet is a decorative, lore world rather than a mining destination. Its signature blocks are the **Sacred wood set** (log, planks, stairs, doors, etc.), **Sacred grass**, and a range of **Sacred flowers** (amaryllis, catharanthus, chrysanthemum, marigold, trillium) and **Sacred ferns**. It has no dedicated ore set.

## Entities

- **Vanilla animals** — pigs, sheep, cows, chickens, rabbits, horses/donkeys, plus fish, axolotls and guardians in the water.
- **Hostiles** — **Mini Buu** and **Endermen**.

## Structures

- **Old Kai's Pillar** — in the Sacred Plains, home of the **Old Kai** (the Elder Kai tied to unlocking your hidden potential — see [[Skills & Abilities|Abilities]]).

## Getting there

Travel by **Space Pod** — see [[Space Pod Destinations|Space-Pod-Destinations]].
