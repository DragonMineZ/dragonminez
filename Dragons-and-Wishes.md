# Dragons & Wishes

DMZ has **two wish-granting dragons**. You summon one by gathering that world's **seven Dragon Balls**, then choosing from its list of wishes. After the wish, the balls turn to stone and re-scatter across the world.

> Dragons, ball sets and wishes are fully **data-driven** — you can add custom dragons, balls and wishes. To edit them see [[Custom Wishes|Custom-Wishes]]. All wishes below are the **defaults**.

## How summoning works

1. **Collect all 7 Dragon Balls** of a set — they generate scattered across the matching world (spawn amount/range are set in [[General Server|general-server]] `worldGen`).
2. Use a **Dragon Radar** to locate them (see [[Items|Items]]).
3. Summon the dragon with the full set and **pick a wish**.
4. The balls scatter again afterwards (controlled by the `generateDragonBalls` toggle).

## Shenron — the Earth Dragon

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| World | **Overworld (Earth)** |
| Balls needed | **7 Earth Dragon Balls** |
| Wishes granted | **1 per summon** |
| Find the balls with | **Dragon Radar** (Earth) — craft from a T1 Radar Chip + T1 Radar CPU |

**Wishes:**

- **16 Senzu Beans**
- **5,000 Training Points**
- The **Power Pole** (Nyoibo)
- **16 Might Tree Fruit**
- **4 Namek Radar CPUs** (T2 Radar CPU)
- A **Saiyan Space Ship**
- **Reset Racial Skill** — clear your racial passive stacks (see [[Skills & Abilities|Abilities]])
- **Re-customize** your character's appearance
- **Change Difficulty**
- **Reset Story** progress
- **Materials pack** (Kikono Shards and more)
- The **Strongest Armor** set (chestplate, leggings, boots)

## Porunga — the Namek Dragon

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| World | **Planet Namek** |
| Balls needed | **7 Namek Dragon Balls** |
| Wishes granted | **3 per summon** |
| Find the balls with | **Namek Dragon Radar** — craft from a T2 Radar Chip + T2 Radar CPU |

**Wishes:**

- **32 Senzu Beans**
- **15,000 Training Points**
- The **Brave Sword**
- **Reset Racial Skill**
- **Re-customize** your character's appearance
- **Relocate Stats** — redistribute your attribute points
- **Change Difficulty**
- **Reset Story** progress
- **Materials pack** (Kikono Shards and more)
- The **Invincible Armor** set (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots)
- The **Invincible Blue Armor** set
- **Potara earrings** (yellow — left & right)
- **Potara earrings** (green — left & right)

Because Porunga grants **three wishes** at once and its balls are on Namek, it's the bigger prize — but you'll need Namek-tier radars and a trip off-world.
