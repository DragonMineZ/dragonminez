# Enchantments

DMZ adds its own enchantments for combat and survival: **armor** enchants that speed up your regeneration, **weapon** enchants that sharpen your attacks, and a pair of rare **Gete-tech** armor enchants only obtainable from a quest.

## Armor — Recovery

Each boosts one regeneration type (see [[Stats & Attributes|Stats-and-Attributes]]). They give diminishing returns per level (~+6.25% per level for the first tier) and stack across your armor pieces. **Only one Recovery type can go on a single piece.**

| Enchantment | Applies to | Max level | Effect |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Vitality Recovery** | Armor | 4 | Faster **Health** regeneration. |
| **Resistance Recovery** | Armor | 4 | Faster **Stamina** regeneration. |
| **Energy Recovery** | Armor | 4 | Faster **Ki / Energy** regeneration. |

## Weapon — Combat

| Enchantment | Applies to | Max level | Effect |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Defense Penetration** | Swords / axes / DMZ weapons | 5 | Ignores **2.5% of the enemy's defense per level** (up to 12.5%). |
| **Healing Reduction** | Swords / axes / DMZ weapons | 4 | Your hits **cut the target's healing** for a few seconds (stacks with the skill of the same name, capped at 40%). |
| **Critical Chance** | Swords / axes / DMZ weapons | 5 | Increases your **critical-hit chance**. |
| **Critical Damage** | Swords / axes / DMZ weapons | 5 | Increases your **critical-hit damage**. |

> **Critical Chance** and **Critical Damage** are mutually exclusive on the same weapon — you pick one crit path per weapon.

## Special — Gete-tech (quest-only)

These two are **not** found in the enchanting table or villager trades. They're rewarded as enchanted books by **Bulma's Gete-tech enhancements** side quest (see [[Custom Quests, Sagas & Sidequests|Custom-Quests-Sagas-and-Sidequests]]).

| Enchantment | Applies to | Max level | Effect |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Ki Conductivity** | Armor | 4 | Boosts your **Ki / Energy regeneration** (on top of Energy Recovery). |
| **Gravity Forged** | Armor | 4 | **Reduces the effect of gravity on you** — at max level it cuts gravity's penalty strength to 40%, letting you shrug off high-gravity worlds like [[Planet Namek|Dimension-Namek]] training or the [[Hyperbolic Time Chamber|Dimension-Hyperbolic-Time-Chamber]]. See [[Gravity System|Gravity-System]]. |
