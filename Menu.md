# Menu

DMZ has two in-game menus: the **Character Menu** (a tabbed window for your stats, skills, quests…) and the **Combat Radial** (a quick wheel for transforming and using skills in a fight).

## Opening them

| Key | Opens |
| :-- | :-- |
| **V** | **Character Menu** (stats, party, skills, sagas, minigames, config) |
| **X** | **Combat Radial** (transformations, skills, actions) |
| **C** | Charge Ki |
| **G** | Action key |
| **H** | Instant Transmission / Space Pod menu |
| **Z** | Lock-on |
| **F** | Ki Sense / Fly |
| **R** | Dash |

(All rebindable in Minecraft's Controls options.)

---

## Character Menu (V)

A tabbed window. Each tab is a button along the menu:

### Stats
Your main character page.
- **Battle Power** readout — your overall power level.
- The **six attribute buttons** — **STR, SKP, RES, VIT, PWR, ENE** — click to **spend attribute points** and raise that stat (see [[Stats & Attributes|Stats-and-Attributes]]).
- **Multiplier / release** info and a **view-switch button** to toggle between the **hexagon** and **bar** stat displays.

### Party
Create and manage your [[party|Commands]] — invite, accept, kick, leave, disband, and toggle party PvP. Party members can share progress and TP.

### Skills
Learn and level your **skills, techniques and transformations** — spend Training Points here. See [[Skills & Abilities|Abilities]] and [[Techniques|Techniques]].

### Sagas / Quests
The **quest tree**: your story-saga progress, objectives and rewards.

> **You can change the difficulty here.** Sagas run on **Easy · Normal · Hard** (Normal by default). Difficulty scales the **enemy stats** (HP/attack) and decides **which rewards unlock** — some rewards only unlock on higher difficulty. In a party, everyone must be on a compatible difficulty to play together. See [[Sagas|Sagas]].

### Minigames
Training minigames — **Rhythm, Control, Memory, Precision, Gravity** and the **Shadow Dummy** — that reward training. See [[Training Points & Mastery|Training-Points-and-Mastery]].

### Config
DMZ client/HUD settings — HUD bar positions, stat-display style, camera and other personal options.

---

## Combat Radial (X)

A radial wheel for the heat of battle. Its nodes let you:

- **Transform** — pick a form from your **form groups** (Super, Legendary, etc.) and power up; **Descend** to power back down.
- **Power Release** — set how much power you're channeling (and your release limit).
- **Racial Skill** — activate your race's signature ability (Zenkai, Assimilation, Drain…). See [[Races|Races]].
- **Ki Actions & Ki Weapons** — fire ki techniques and form ki weapons.
- **Movement & skill toggles** — toggle flight and other togglable skills, and layer **stack forms** like Kaioken.
