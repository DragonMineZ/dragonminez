# Techniques

The combat techniques you can learn from **[[Masters|Structures]]**. There are three kinds:

- **Ki Attacks** — ranged energy attacks; their damage scales with **Ki Power (PWR)**.
- **Strike Attacks** — close-range rush/technique attacks; their damage scales with **Strike Power (SKP)**.
- **Special Attacks** — utility/support techniques that deal no direct damage (control, support, buffs).

Each is learned from a specific master and costs training points (TP) to unlock. See [[Stats & Attributes|Stats-and-Attributes]] for how PWR/SKP work, and [[Skills|Skills]] to retune costs/eligibility.

> ⭐ **You need Ki Control.** Every technique here relies on channeling ki, so the **Ki Control** skill is a prerequisite — without it you can't charge or use techniques at all. Learn it before spending TP on attacks (see [[Skills & Abilities|Abilities]]).

### Support techniques

Beyond the fixed techniques above, DMZ's **technique system** lets techniques be built for **support** instead of damage:

- **Heal** — a technique can be set to restore health instead of dealing damage (available on **Area**, **Small Ball** and **Shield** ki types). Great for healers/Clerics.
- **Buff** — a technique's secondary effect can **buff** the caster/allies rather than debuff enemies.

These pair well with the **Spiritualist** and **Cleric** classes (see [[Player Classes|Player-Classes]]), whose passives boost ki-technique cooldowns and durations.

> Costs and which master teaches what are the **defaults** and can be changed in `skills.json` — see [[Skills|Skills]].
